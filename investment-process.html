Overview / Investment Process
Methodology

The investment process

Conviction becomes a portfolio through process. The managers study regulatory friction, potential deregulation, and company fundamentals to identify U.S. businesses they consider positioned for a shift toward freer-market conditions.

01

Target

Focus on sectors where government rules, permitting, mandates, capital requirements, and licensing can materially shape business conditions.

02

Score

Evaluate companies through a free-market lens, combining regulatory sensitivity with financial metrics that measure efficiency, profitability, and adaptability.

03

Build

Construct a high-conviction portfolio of approximately 30–50 companies the managers believe fit the Fund's active free-market framework.

An active, multi-faceted process

The process is active and multi-faceted. Rather than tracking an index, the management team conducts ongoing research into regulatory developments, free-market policy shifts, and company fundamentals, seeking businesses with regulatory sensitivity, adaptability, and strong financial characteristics.

Seven financial metrics

In the Score stage, the managers apply a methodology built on seven key financial metrics that measure efficiency, profitability, and adaptability. The specific metrics and weightings are described in the Fund’s materials.
