Risks & disclosures

Investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal. The summaries below are provided for diligence in this prototype and must be confirmed against the current prospectus before any production use.

Deregulation Strategy Risks Summary - confirm vs. prospectus

The Fund’s strategy of investing in companies that may benefit from deregulatory measures entails significant risks, including those stemming from the unpredictable nature of regulatory and political trends. Deregulation is influenced by political, economic, and social factors, which can shift rapidly and in unforeseen directions. Changes in government priorities, political leadership, or public sentiment may result in the reversal of existing deregulatory policies or the introduction of new regulations. Not all targeted companies may achieve the expected advantages, whether fully, partially, or at all.

Equity Market Risk

The equity securities held in the Fund’s portfolio may experience sudden, unpredictable drops in value or long periods of decline. Common stocks are generally exposed to greater risk than other types of securities. Factors that could impact market value include a company’s business performance, investor perceptions, stock market trends, and general economic conditions.

New Fund & New Sub-Adviser Risk

The Fund is a recently organized management investment company with a limited operating history, so prospective investors do not have a track record on which to base decisions. TRM is a newly registered investment adviser and has not previously served as an adviser or sub-adviser to an investment company.

High Portfolio Turnover Risk

The Fund may actively and frequently trade all or a significant portion of its portfolio. A high turnover rate increases transaction costs and may cause adverse tax consequences due to an increase in short-term capital gains.

Underlying ETF & Digital Asset Exposure Risk

The Fund may invest up to 5% of its net assets in U.S.-listed exchange-traded products seeking exposure to Bitcoin or Ether; it does not invest directly in Bitcoin, Ether, or any other digital assets. Investing in such products carries significant risks, including high market volatility, and the risks of blockchain technology, which remains a relatively new and largely untested innovation.
