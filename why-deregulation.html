The Investment Thesis

Why deregulation matters

Deregulation investing is the idea that companies can be affected—positively or negatively—by shifts in the regulatory environment. FMKT is designed to provide focused exposure to U.S. companies the managers believe are positioned to benefit from regulatory relief.

The cost of regulation

Excessive regulation costs American businesses an estimated $2.1 trillion annually— approximately $15,000 per U.S. household, according to public fund materials. These costs touch compliance, operations, and the pace at which businesses can innovate.

The Fund’s thesis is that when regulatory burdens are reduced, companies often experience changes in operational efficiency, profitability, and innovation. FMKT seeks to identify and provide exposure to companies across sectors that stand to benefit most from regulatory relief.

A first-of-its-kind focus

According to its public materials, The Free Markets ETF is the first ETF specifically designed to focus on the economic dynamics created by deregulation. It provides focused exposure to U.S. companies positioned to benefit from shifts in the regulatory environment that support free-market dynamics.

Sectors in focus

The Fund concentrates its research on sectors the managers identify as carrying high regulatory cost burdens and compelling policy momentum:

HealthcareFinancial ServicesEnergyTransportationTechnology

Important context

Deregulation is influenced by political, economic, and social factors that can shift rapidly and in unforeseen directions. Existing policies may be reversed and new regulations introduced. The statements above describe the Fund’s strategy and thesis; they are not predictions of Fund results or returns. See Risks & Disclosures.
